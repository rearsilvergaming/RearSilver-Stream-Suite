**Twitch streamers — want to help test RearSilver Stream Suite?**

I've been building a suite that combines an **OBS dock** for quick stream controls with a **Control Hub** for music, viewer requests, overlays and stream tools. I'm opening up a **free private beta** and looking for streamers willing to try it and tell me how it fits into their setup.

**What's inside?**
• OBS dock controls, scenes/sources and live stats.
• Local music, YouTube/YouTube Music fallback playlists, and optional Spotify desktop integration.
• Twitch song requests, configurable command permissions, and optional bot-account messages.
• A customisable now-playing overlay.
• Instant Replay with a frame designer, Quick Text presets, and Timer/Countdown tools.
• Browser Refresh, App Manager, and guided setup.

You don't need testing experience. I'm interested in **bugs, confusing wording, awkward setup steps, and anything that would make the Suite more useful during your streams**. Honest feedback is the point of the beta.

**You'll need Windows and OBS Studio.** Spotify is optional; its integration requires Premium, the desktop app and your own Spotify developer app.

**Apply here:** https://imperialinfusions.com/pages/rearsilver-private-beta

Applications are reviewed manually. Approved testers will receive the private access details and beta Discord invite.
