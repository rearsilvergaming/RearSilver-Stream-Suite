Request content safety

The Suite applies every available check, but YouTube can still make a different runtime decision and no filter can prevent every attempted bypass.

Search filteringStrict is recommended for family-friendly streams. Moderate permits a broader range of search results for mature streams.
Music category onlyAdds an independent filtering layer that may reject non-music or mature content missed by other safeguards. YouTube categories are uploader-assigned, so legitimate music may occasionally be rejected and unsuitable content cannot be guaranteed to be blocked.

OBS Music Capture and copyright

Excluding music from the Twitch VOD track can help prevent automated VOD muting and claims, but it does not grant permission to broadcast music live. Rights holders and platforms may still restrict or act on its use. The Suite never changes stream, recording or VOD audio routing automatically.
