App Manager

OBS & Overlays

Choose how Suite-managed sources are organised and review the OBS-native components they need.

Overlay placement

Choose where managed stream tools are placed when they next create or repair their OBS placement.

Existing placements are never deleted

Managed infrastructure

Individual tools create and repair their own sources when used. Changing mode affects their next placement repair rather than destructively moving existing sources.

Checking OBS connection

Music Capture

Create or configure the neutral OBS Application Audio Capture used by the music system. The Suite never changes stream, recording or VOD tracks.

In OBS source properties, choose RearSilver Stream Suite Control Hub for built-in YouTube or local playback. Choose Spotify or your preferred external music player when using external playback. The Suite will preserve your selection.
